About

Hi, I'm Casey.

I build websites because I like solving real problems for real people—and because a good website should feel like it was actually made for the business it represents.

Why I do this work

I'm Casey Keown, the developer behind Custom Web Architecture. I build fast, mobile-friendly websites and practical web applications for small businesses, nonprofits, and organizations that need their online presence to do real work—not just look nice.

I like the craft of it: figuring out what a business actually needs, then building something that fits. No mystery tech talk, no disappearing after launch, and no generic template passed off as a custom solution.

A direct approach

You work with me, not a rotating account team. That means clear communication, realistic timelines, and someone who actually remembers the details of your project from one conversation to the next.

My approach comes from a career where details matter: understand the objective, communicate clearly, and finish the job. I build websites outside my full-time public-service career, and I bring that same attention to detail and follow-through to every project.

Outside of work

My faith, family, and commitment to treating people well shape how I run my business. I'm a husband, a father of two, an animal lover, and a musician—I play drums, and if you look closely you might spot a small nod to that around the site.

Who I'd like to work with

Business owners, founders, nonprofits, and community organizations with a good story and a website that doesn't tell it yet. If that's you, I'd like to hear about it.
