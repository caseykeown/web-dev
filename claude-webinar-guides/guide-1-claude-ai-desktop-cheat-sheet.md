# Guide 1: The Claude.ai & Desktop App Cheat Sheet

*Your friendly map to everywhere Claude lives, and how to get around faster.*

---

## Welcome!

If you're brand new to Claude, congratulations. You've already done the hardest part: showing up. This guide walks you through the two main "front doors" to Claude, shows you how to collaborate with other people, and gives you a cheat sheet of shortcuts you'll use every single day.

No technical background needed. Let's go!

---

## Part 1: Claude.ai (Web) vs. the Claude Desktop App

Both are made by Anthropic, both talk to the same Claude, and both use the same account and the same subscription. The difference is *where* they live and *what extras* they unlock.

### Claude.ai, the Web Interface

* **What it is:** Claude in your browser. Go to **claude.ai**, sign in, and start chatting.
* **Works on:** Any device with a web browser, including a laptop, tablet, phone, or a borrowed computer.
* **Nothing to install.** If you can open a website, you can use Claude.
* **Best for:** Everyday conversations, writing and brainstorming, uploading documents and images, using Projects, and creating Artifacts (documents, web pages, and visuals that Claude builds for you in a side panel).

### The Claude Desktop App

* **What it is:** A dedicated app you download and install on your Mac or Windows computer from **claude.ai/download**.
* **Everything the website does, plus:**
  * **Always one keystroke away.** A global keyboard shortcut summons Claude from any app, so you never have to hunt for a browser tab.
  * **Deeper integration with your computer.** With your permission, the desktop app can work alongside files and folders that live on your machine, not just files you upload into a chat.
  * **It feels like a real app.** It sits in your dock or taskbar, sends native notifications, and stays signed in.
* **Best for:** People who reach for Claude many times a day and want it woven into their workflow.

### Quick Comparison

| Feature | Claude.ai (Web) | Desktop App |
|---|---|---|
| Installation | None, just a browser | Download for Mac or Windows |
| Use it on any device | Yes | Only where you installed it |
| Global "summon Claude" shortcut | No | Yes |
| Works with local files and folders | Uploads only | Yes, with your permission |
| Projects, Artifacts, Connectors | Yes | Yes |

> **Beginner's rule of thumb:** Start on **claude.ai** in your browser. Once Claude becomes part of your daily routine, install the desktop app for speed and deeper integration. There are also mobile apps for iPhone and Android, using the same account and showing the same conversations.

## Part 2: Collaborating with Claude and Your Team

### Claude Projects: Your Organized Workspaces

Think of a **Project** as a folder with a memory. Instead of re-explaining your business, your class, or your novel at the start of every new chat, you set it up once.

**How to create a Project:**

1. In the sidebar on claude.ai, click **Projects**.
2. Click **New Project** and give it a clear name, such as "Q4 Marketing," "Wedding Planning," or "History Thesis."
3. Add **Project knowledge**: upload documents, paste in reference text, or write notes that Claude should always keep in mind.
4. Optionally, add **custom instructions**, for example: "Always write in a warm, plain-spoken tone" or "Our company is Nalls Hauling, based in Kentucky."
5. Start any new chat *inside* that Project. Claude automatically knows everything you've added.

**Why beginners love Projects:**

* No more copying and pasting the same background info into every chat.
* All related conversations stay grouped in one place.
* Update the knowledge once, and every future chat uses the new version.

### Sharing Artifacts

An **Artifact** is a standalone piece of work that Claude creates, such as a formatted document, an interactive web page, a diagram, a quiz, or a small app. It appears in its own panel beside the chat.

**To share an Artifact:**

1. When Claude creates an Artifact, look for the **Share** or **Publish** option on the Artifact panel.
2. Publish it to get a **link** you can send to anyone. They see the finished product, not your whole conversation.
3. If you improve the Artifact later, publish again so your existing link shows the newest version.

> Artifacts are perfect for handing off deliverables. Send a colleague the polished one-page guide, not a wall of chat text.

### Team Coworking (Team and Enterprise Plans)

If your organization uses a **Claude Team or Enterprise plan**, collaboration goes further:

* **Shared Projects.** Make a Project available to teammates so everyone works from the same knowledge base and the same instructions.
* **Shared Artifacts.** Publish an Artifact so it's visible to your organization. A report or tool one person builds becomes usable by everybody.
* **Central billing and admin.** One workspace, managed seats, and organization-wide settings, with no individual subscriptions to juggle.

**Beginner takeaway:** Even on an individual plan, Projects plus shared Artifact links give you most of the collaboration magic.

## Part 3: The Daily Cheat Sheet

### Keyboard Shortcuts You'll Actually Use

*On Windows, use **Ctrl** wherever Mac uses **Cmd**.*

| Shortcut | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Enter** | Send your message |
| **Shift + Enter** | Add a new line without sending, which is great for lists and longer prompts |
| **Cmd/Ctrl + K** | Open quick search to jump to any past conversation or start a new one |
| **Esc** | Stop Claude mid-response if it's heading the wrong direction |
| **Copy button on a code block** | Grab code cleanly without selecting it by hand |
| **Option + Space** (Mac, desktop app default) | Summon Claude from anywhere on your computer |

> Shortcuts occasionally change as the interface evolves. Poke around the in-app help or settings to see the current list on your device.

### Interface Tips That Feel Like Superpowers

* **Edit and re-ask.** Hover over a message *you* sent and click **Edit**. Claude answers again from that point, which is perfect for refining a prompt without starting over.
* **Retry for a different take.** Not thrilled with a response? Use retry to get a fresh version.
* **Rename your chats.** Click a conversation's title and rename it something findable. "Invoice template draft" beats "Untitled."
* **Upload almost anything.** Drag and drop PDFs, Word documents, spreadsheets, and images straight into the chat box.
* **Use your voice.** On the mobile apps, tap the microphone and just talk.
* **Pick the right model.** The model selector near the chat box lets you choose faster models for quick tasks and more powerful ones for complex work.
* **Star what matters.** Favorite or pin important conversations so they're easy to find later.

## Pro-Tip

**Create a "Personal Assistant" Project on day one.** Add a short document describing who you are, what you do, and how you like things written, for example: "I run a small hauling business in Kentucky. Keep emails short and friendly." Then start your everyday chats inside that Project. It's the fastest way to make Claude's answers feel like they were made for you, because they were.
