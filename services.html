Services

Website work that fits what your business actually needs.

No mystery tech talk, and no bundle of features you'll never use. Here's what I build, in plain language.

Starter Landing Pages

A single, well-built page for a business that needs a professional online presence without a full multi-page site. Good for new businesses, events, or a simple "here's who we are and how to reach us."

Small-Business Websites

A complete multi-page site—home, services, about, contact, and whatever else your business needs—built around your goals and your customers.

Website Redesigns

If your current site is outdated, hard to update, or doesn't represent your business well anymore, I'll rebuild it with a clearer structure and a design that fits today.

Mobile Optimization

Most visitors arrive on a phone. Every site I build is designed mobile-first, and I can also improve an existing site that doesn't work well on smaller screens.

Search-Engine Fundamentals

Clear page titles, useful descriptions, sensible structure, and technical cleanup so search engines can find and understand your site. No guarantees, no gimmicks—just solid fundamentals.

Content Management Setup

If you want to make your own updates after launch, I'll set up a system you can actually use, and walk you through how it works.

Website Maintenance

Ongoing updates, repairs, and small changes after launch for clients who want a website that's looked after, not abandoned.

Social Link & One-Page Sites

A fast, branded alternative to Linktree-style bio pages—your own domain, your own design, no platform limitations.

Custom Applications

Focused tools that automate repetitive work or organize information for a specialized workflow, built when an off-the-shelf option doesn't fit.

How much does a website cost?

It depends on scope. A simple landing page costs less than a full multi-page business site with custom features. I'll give you a clear, specific quote after we talk about what you need—no surprise line items later. Read more about typical pricing.

Do I need to know anything about web development?

No. I'll explain what your website needs, and what it does not, in plain English at every step.

What if I already have a website?

That's fine. I can redesign an existing site, migrate it to a better platform, or take over maintenance, depending on what you need.

Do you work with businesses outside Kentucky?

My primary service area covers Hardin, Grayson, Breckinridge, Meade, LaRue, Bullitt, and Nelson Counties, plus the Louisville Metro Area, but I also work remotely with good people anywhere.
