Casey's Blurbs

About me:

I'm Casey Keown, the developer behind Custom Web Architecture. I build fast, mobile-friendly websites and practical web applications for small businesses, nonprofits, and organizations that need their online presence to do real work.

My approach comes from a career where details matter: understand the objective, account for the evidence, communicate clearly, and finish the job. That means no mystery tech talk, no disappearing after launch, and no generic template passed off as a custom solution.

I serve clients throughout Kentucky and work remotely with good people anywhere. If you have a project in mind, tell me what you're building.

Who I'd like to meet:

Business owners, founders, nonprofits, artists, and community organizations with a good story and a website that does not tell it yet.
