How it works

A simple, honest process

  1. Tell me about your business.

    We start with a conversation about what you do, who you serve, and what your website needs to accomplish.

  2. We choose a practical plan.

    I recommend an approach that fits your goals and budget—nothing more complicated than you actually need.

  3. I design and build the site.

    You'll see progress along the way, not silence until the big reveal.

  4. You review it and provide feedback.

    We refine the details together until it's right.

  5. I launch it and help you understand what comes next.

    You'll know how to update your site and who to call if something needs attention.
